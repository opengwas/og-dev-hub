Release notes

Every ready Analysis in the hybrid__European candidate pool of the EBI GWAS Catalog harmonised GWAS-SSF Source Collection, resolved through AF-based ancestry assignment and phenotype-SD effect-scale estimation. Mixed study designs: quantitative Analyses carry an empirically estimated phenotype SD, and case-control Analyses are stored on the log-OR scale with SD estimation explicitly skipped rather than defaulted.

The candidate pool is 6,219 Analyses (GWAS Catalog release 2026-11-19). Only the subset the frozen Source Inventory records as ready becomes release membership; the remaining inputs stay inventory facts and are never selected. Two accession pairs are byte-identical under different accessions and are reported for an explicit review decision instead of being silently collapsed (`sidecars/source_readiness.tsv` and `duplicates` in the preflight report). Release Erratum (issue #478, 2026-11-22): this bundle replaces the one the OGS-00011 Store published on 2026-11-01 was built from, on the owner’s decision of 2026-11-22 to re-emit and rebuild it under its own id. It is re-resolved at opengwasdb 0.14.1 from the GWAS Catalog’s 2026-11-19 release (#491). Membership moves from 3,169 to 3,245 Analyses. Thirteen leave, refused at resolution: GCST002047, GCST004461 and GCST90165257, whose effect contradicts their own p-value (#403, #412, ADR 0069), the Store having held statistics read from a mislabelled effect column; GCST90014299 to GCST90014304, GCST90444480 and GCST90833021, whose p-value test is MR-MEGA (opengwasdb#371, ADR 0085); and GCST90572740 and GCST90572741, whose beta/se is deflated against their own p-value (ADR 0069). Eighty-nine join: 66 of the 103 Analyses new to the catalogue; 19 previously excluded as ancestry_unassigned whose sources EBI has since re-harmonised; GCST90278455 and GCST90278456, previously ancestry_unassigned, whose sources are unchanged and whose ancestry residual now falls below the gate; and GCST90627758 and GCST90627773, which the catalogue now records as quantitative. The Dense axis is the reference-first gnomAD generation (opengwasdb ADR 0079, #381): a deletion is spelt reference first, so its z has the opposite sign to the published Store’s, and a deletion and an insertion at one position are two variants. The rebuild writes format 0.2.3, which stores an SE beyond float16’s range as missing and counts it (opengwasdb ADR 0068), so the eight +inf SE cells of the 2026-11-01 erratum (#401) are not stored again. #225’s on-rsid-conflict: drop and hm3 overflow-only: skip are carried. Per Analysis, #478 refuses rather than remaps. GCST90165257 cannot be remapped: its standard_error is NA throughout. GCST002047 (odds ratios in `beta`) and GCST004461 (log-odds in `odds_ratio`) could be read through a Column Map of their own, but a release reads one Column Map and the resolver takes one per run, so remapping them is left as a separate change. #487’s gwas-ssf-no-p Column Map is not carried. Without it GCST90446466 is refused at resolution as `resolution_failed`, and the recorded detail calls its z deflated; that is the effect-consistency check reading its `p_value`, which holds 1 - p/2 (owner ruling of 2 Nov 2026, opengwasdb#370), not a fault in its beta/se. Through the map it resolves at 0.14.1 but fails the ancestry gate (residual 0.219 against 0.06), so it is excluded either way. Until the rebuild publishes, the Store served as OGS-00011 is the 3,169-Analysis build of 2026-10-26/27, completed and validated on 2026-11-01, from the bundle committed at 373f9a0f, whose notes carry its errata (#225, #401, #403, #412). In that Store the statistics of GCST002047, GCST004461 and GCST90165257, and every cell Reference Completion imputed from them, are wrong and must not be used.

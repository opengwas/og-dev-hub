When a source’s p-value and its beta/se disagree

A Store keeps two statistics per association: z (= beta / se) and SE (spec §6). It never keeps the source’s p-value. The p a query returns is the two-sided normal p of the stored z (opengwasdb/query/resolve.py, log10_p_two_sided). That is exact for a Wald test, which is what most sources report. Wherever a source’s p is not the Wald p of its own beta/se, the Store answers with a p the source never published.

The source’s p is read once, at resolve time, as a witness against beta/se: the effect-consistency check (its contract, ADR 0069). Each Analysis first declares which test its p comes from (p_value_test, ADR 0085): - wald_1df is checked; - joint_2df skips the check; - mr_mega is refused unread.

The check compares the two over a 20,000-row sample and refuses an Analysis on either of two medians: - the median |Δ| is above 1.0 (#345); - the median signed Δ is above +0.1 (#370).

Here Δ = log10 p implied by z − log10 p reported. A median sees an Analysis that is wrong throughout. It cannot see: - a fault confined to some rows; - a p that is legitimately from a different test, but close.

This page lists every circumstance measured so far, on the 4,783 sources of OGS-00011 read whole (#357, #371; evidence in the issue threads). For each it gives which number is right and what the engine does today. Each row names the test that pins that behaviour: - catalogue: <name> is a case in tests/test_statistic_disagreement_catalogue.py, built from the circumstance’s mechanism; - other test names are in tests/test_resolve_effect_consistency.py.

A test that pins “stored” is deliberate. It records a gap, and it changes when the gap is closed.

Terms. - A false hit has stored |z| ≥ 5.45 (p 5e-8), where the source’s p is above 1e-4. - A lost hit has stored |z| < 3.72, where the source’s p is at most 5e-8. - The shares and medians below are whole-file unless they are marked otherwise.

Not yet explained

  • GCST90292553 (the CAMP method, Ojavee et al. 2023): median |Δ| 0.343, signed −0.343, and 0 false hits among 30,571 hits.
  • GCST006979: carries a second p column; median |Δ| 0.189, and 106 false hits among 131,534.

Both are stored.

What closes the gaps

  • #371 measured the second table on whole files.
    • No threshold on the share of false hits separates MR-MEGA from the rest. MR-MEGA spans 13.5–99.9%, and four Analyses that are not MR-MEGA sit inside that range.
    • What closed it is a declaration (p_value_test, ADR 0085), because what test a p comes from is a fact about the Analysis that no statistic of the file recovers. The declaration is trusted, and nothing checks it.
  • Rows that go wrong at low frequency are a matter of admission per Analysis (a MAF threshold), not of this check. The thresholds are the Source Collection’s, chosen from #371’s measurement.
  • Still open: an SE printed with too few digits, the two unexplained sources above, and a Store that does not say which of its Analyses report a joint-test p.
